# How we work (10 min)
**Goal:** know who does what, and the seven habits we'll practise.

Claude writes the code. **You decide, predict and check.** You won't build this alone afterwards,
and that's fine: the aim is to know every part, the words for it, and how to direct an agent.

## The seven habits
1. **Measure first.** Decide how you'll judge success before building.
2. **Start dumb.** A model is only as good as its margin over the simplest baseline.
3. **Predict, then run.** The surprises are where you learn.
4. **One change at a time.** Record every experiment.
5. **Small gains are often noise.** Re-run with another seed before believing them.
6. **Too good to be true?** It's usually leakage.
7. **Look at the data and the mistakes**, not only the averages.
