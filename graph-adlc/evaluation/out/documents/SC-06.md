## REQ-LOW-STOCK-ALERT — Low-stock alert
Kind: requirement · Revision: r1

Merchandising is alerted when on-hand stock of a SKU falls below 10 units. Alerts are produced from the reservation events emitted by CMP-INVENTORY.

- acceptance criteria:
  - An alert is raised when on-hand stock drops below 10 units
  - At most one alert per SKU per day
- assumptions: (none)

## REQ-PLACE-ORDER — Place an order
Kind: requirement · Revision: r1

A customer places an order for in-stock items. The order service reserves stock through the inventory API before accepting the order and shows the order total.

- acceptance criteria:
  - An order is accepted only after a successful reservation
  - The order total is shown with two decimal places
- assumptions: (none)

Relationships:
- Refines parent CAP-ORDERING [O03]

## REQ-RESERVE-STOCK — Reserve stock atomically
Kind: requirement · Revision: r1

Stock is reserved atomically per SKU; a reservation expires after 15 minutes if the order is not confirmed.

- acceptance criteria:
  - Two concurrent reservations never oversell a SKU
  - Unconfirmed reservations expire after 15 minutes
- assumptions: (none)

Relationships:
- Refines parent CAP-ORDERING [O04]
- Constrained by CON-INVENTORY-API [O08]
- Implemented by CMP-INVENTORY [O15]
- Verified by TEST-RESERVE [O28]
