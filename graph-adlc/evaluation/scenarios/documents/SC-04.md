## REQ-RETRY — Retry transient payment failures
Kind: requirement · Revision: r2

Retry only transient gateway errors up to three attempts, using bounded exponential backoff and a stable idempotency key.

- acceptance criteria:
  - At most three attempts are made
  - Backoff increases between retries with a configured upper limit
  - Every attempt uses the original idempotency key
  - Permanent failures are not retried
- assumptions: (none)
